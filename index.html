Frequently Asked

How long does a smile makeover take?

Typically, a smile makeover can take between 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the complexity of the treatments involved. We provide a detailed timeline during your initial consultation.

Do you offer financing options?

Yes, we offer several flexible financing plans to ensure that your dream smile is accessible. Our team can discuss these options with you during your visit.

Is the treatment painful?

Your comfort is our priority. We use the latest anesthesia techniques and offer sedation options for a completely stress-free and painless experience.
